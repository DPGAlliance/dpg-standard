# DPGs for AI Collection

A DPGA Collection that highlights Digital Public Goods enabling the **ethical and responsible development of AI** in support of the SDGs, with particular attention to low-resource contexts and underrepresented domains.

**Status:** 🟡 Draft criteria, open for community input
**Co-stewards & partners:** Digital Public Goods Alliance (DPGA), with input from the [Open Data Institute (ODI)](https://theodi.org/), the [United Nations International Computing Centre (UNICC)](https://www.unicc.org/), and [Digital Futures Lab](https://digitalfutureslab.in/).

## Contents

- [`criteria.md`](./criteria.md) — the technical criteria a DPG must meet to be included (Layers 1–3).
- [`CHANGELOG.md`](./CHANGELOG.md) — version history of the criteria.

## Objectives

- **Elevate visibility.** Amplify DPGs driving the ethical and responsible development of AI to support the SDGs in an equitable way.
- **Illustrate impact.** Feature adoption stories and best practices showing how DPGs enable responsible AI development.
- **Map the ecosystem.** Provide an overview of AI-relevant DPGs to help navigate the open-source ecosystem, especially for low-resource contexts and underrepresented domains.

## How the criteria are built

The collection assesses three categories of DPG — **open source software, open data, and open AI systems** — through a layered model:

1. **Layer 1 — Prerequisite:** the solution is a recognized DPG in the [DPG Registry](https://www.digitalpublicgoods.net/registry).
2. **Layer 2 — AI relevance:** a single relevance gate (category fit, AI-lifecycle utility, documented relevance/impact).
3. **Layer 3 — Technical criteria:** category-specific mandatory criteria, with recommended best practices surfaced separately.

Inclusion is a **binary gate** based on mandatory criteria only; recommended criteria are shown as best practices met, not as a score. Full detail is in [`criteria.md`](./criteria.md).

## What we're looking for

This is a working draft. We would value community input on, in particular:

- **Mandatory vs. recommended placement** — is anything in the mandatory tier too demanding for Global Majority maintainers, or anything in the recommended tier that should be mandatory?
- **Evidence expectations** — are the named evidence artifacts (datasheets, model cards, Croissant/Croissant-RAI, benchmark reports) realistic and assessable from public documentation?
- **Category boundaries** — are the Layer 2 inclusions/exclusions for software, data, and AI systems drawn in the right place?
- **Gaps** — dimensions or risks the criteria miss, especially for low-resource and non-English contexts.
- **Overlap** — anywhere the criteria unintentionally duplicate the DPG Standard or the Maturity Model rather than complementing them.
